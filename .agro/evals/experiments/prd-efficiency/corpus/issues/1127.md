Langfuse now publishes official plugins for Claude Code, Pi, and Codex. #1125 rewrote the documentation around them. Two surfaces from the superseded community `pi-langfuse` fork remain in the tree and no documentation references either one.

**1. `.pi/install/install-langfuse.sh`**

The helper installs a pinned fork commit of the community `pi-langfuse` package, applies an OpenTelemetry override, and runs an audit. `@langfuse/pi-observability-plugin` replaces it. Remove the helper, its test, and its `.pi/install/README.md` row.

**2. The `langfuse` section of `agro.json`**

`langfuse.baseUrl` and `langfuse.privacyPreset` reach no process. `config-render.ts` already lists both names in `RETIRED_KEYS` and refuses to render them. No official plugin reads a privacy preset. Operators set `LANGFUSE_BASE_URL` in the harness environment instead.

Remove the schema, the two `agro config set` paths, and the two `docs/configuration.md` rows. Keep the `RETIRED_KEYS` entries so a stale compose variable still fails loudly.

Configuration validation ignores unknown sections, so an existing `agro.json` that carries a `langfuse` section keeps loading.
