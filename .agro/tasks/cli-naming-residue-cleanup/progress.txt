THE SWEEP GAP -- why this class survived US-001, US-004 and US-009.
  Every sweep on this task, the advisor's included, matched
  `oh <lifecycle-verb>`. A sentence saying "the `oh` CLI" with NO VERB after it
  was never in any candidate set. The operator identified this; it is upstream of
  the workers and upstream of the advisor's own classifier.
  docs/intro.md is the project front door and states it three times, in the same
  paragraph that correctly says `agro sandbox install docker`. That is #1046's
  headline defect restated in prose, shipped past three passes.
  LESSON: a rename sweep must be derived from at least two orthogonal patterns —
  one anchored on the verb that follows the binary, one anchored on the binary
  alone. A single regex shape defines a blind spot the size of everything it does
  not match, and no amount of careful classification inside that set can see out
  of it.
